# Big Odds Prediction App – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

Big Odds Prediction App ("the app") shows daily soccer and NBA predictions. This policy explains what data is
handled when you use the app.

## Data we collect
We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:
- your device's advertising ID,
- device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
- your IP address (used to estimate a general region),
- ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or a rewarded video was watched).

This data is collected by Google, not by us. See how Google uses data:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of
personalised ads, in your Android settings under Google > Ads.

## Predictions data
The app downloads the daily predictions file from GitHub. GitHub may log your IP address when the file is
downloaded, as any website does.

## Children
The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

## Responsible gambling
Predictions are opinions and are not guarantees. The app does not take bets or handle money.
